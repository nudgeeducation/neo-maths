NEO Mathematics: How Much Is a Digit Worth?

Driving Question: Can the same digit represent completely different amounts?

Foundations: Number Sense & Operations — place value, estimation and integers.

Stepping Stones: extend place-value understanding beyond earlier stages; recognise the tenfold relationship between neighbouring columns; interpret placeholder zeros; partition and recombine numbers; and move fluently between numerals, words, expanded form and visual representations.

Curriculum connection: understand and use place value for decimals, measures and integers of any size; order numbers; move between numerical representations; and select appropriate representations to support reasoning and problem-solving.

🌱 Open Learner Guide 📘 Open Supporting Adult Guidance
🌿 Connection — What relationships give a digit its value?

What gives a digit its value?

A digit’s value depends on its position. Each place-value column is ten times the value of the column immediately to its right.

Millions Hundred thousands Ten thousands Thousands Hundreds Tens Ones . Tenths Hundredths Thousandths

In 4,070,205.036, the first 4 represents four million. The 7 represents seventy thousand, the 2 represents two hundred, the 3 represents three hundredths and the 6 represents six thousandths.

Placeholder zeros

A zero can show that a particular place-value column contains no units. Removing it can change the number completely.

4.08 is not the same as 4.8.
The zero in 4.08 keeps the 8 in the hundredths column.

Key vocabulary

digit, value, place value, integer, decimal, placeholder, partition, recombine, expanded form, ten times, one tenth

🌿 Connection — Who depends on accurate place value?

Place value and responsible budgeting

Community organisations depend on accurate budgets. A misplaced digit or missing zero could change a cost by ten, one hundred or even one thousand times.

A cost entered as £4,205.70 is very different from £420.57. In the first number, the digit 2 represents £200. In the second, it represents £20.

Connect the mathematics to decisions and responsibility

  • Who might be affected by a place-value error in a budget?
  • Why should numerical information be checked before decisions are made?
  • How could clear place-value notation support trust?

🌙 Rest — How can I make a crowded number small enough to enter?

Pause and locate the digit

Large numbers can feel crowded. You do not need to process the whole number at once. Slow the number down and inspect one column at a time.

  1. Locate the decimal point.
  2. Find the selected digit.
  3. Name its place-value column.
  4. State its value.
  5. Check one neighbouring column for the tenfold relationship.

Helpful self-talk: “I do not need to understand the whole number at once. I can locate one digit and reason from its column.”

You may pause here, close the other panels, use paper or the Scratchpad if available, and return with just one digit in focus.

🍎 Nutrition — What does this place-value work feed?

Mode B: intellectual nourishment

There is no honest food, body or health context inside place value, so we do not invent one. What this lesson feeds instead is the way you think about number: precision (knowing exactly what each digit is worth), a sense of structure (seeing the columns as a repeating tenfold pattern rather than a row of separate symbols), and confidence with scale (staying calm whether a number is in the millions or the thousandths).

Notice, gently: after today, does a long number feel a little less crowded than it did? Can you locate a single digit and say what it is worth without re-reading the whole number? If so, that is the nourishment — clearer thinking you can carry into every later topic that uses number.

Carry one idea forward: every column is ten times the one to its right. Hold on to that one relationship and much of the rest of number will keep making sense.

I can choose a representation, check my work and explain what helped me learn. I can work independently, refine an explanation, compare valid representations and justify which is most effective.
