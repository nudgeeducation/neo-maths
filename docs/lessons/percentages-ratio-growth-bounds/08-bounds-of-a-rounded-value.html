Bounds of a rounded value

Driving Question: What could it have been before rounding?

Lesson type: Core — a rounded value stands for a whole interval.

Stepping Stone: find the lower and upper bounds of a value rounded to a given degree of accuracy, and write its error interval, such as 7.25 ≤ x < 7.35.

You will need: rounding to decimal places and significant figures, plus the optional ✏️ Scratchpad.

🔤 The idea for this lesson

A rounded value stands for every value that rounds to it. The smallest is the lower bound; the upper bound is where the values stop rounding to it. Both are half a unit of the rounding away: a length of 7.3 cm to 1 decimal place could be anything from 7.25 cm up to, but not including, 7.35 cm. Write this as an error interval: 7.25 ≤ x < 7.35.

What to do: choose how a value was rounded and set the rounded value. Move the probe along the line and read what each value rounds to. Find where the values stop rounding to it, then press Show the interval. Try Zoom out to see the neighbouring intervals.

What you are trying to notice: how far the edges are from the rounded value, and which edge is included.

🍎 Nutrition — What this feeds

Mode: practical. Today the mathematics feeds how you read measurements.

What it feeds: today feeds reading measurements honestly. A parcel “2 kg to the nearest kg”, a sign saying “3 miles”, or a crowd of “40 000” are all rounded. Knowing the bounds tells you how much they could really be, which matters when you check a limit, plan a journey or compare two figures.

Carry one idea forward: rounded to what?

🛠️ Final Task — Bounds around you

Your task: find three rounded measurements around you or in the news (a distance sign, a height, a population, a time).

  • For each, say what it was probably rounded to (its degree of accuracy).
  • Find its lower and upper bounds, and write its error interval.
  • Draw one of them on a number line, with a filled dot at the lower bound and an open dot at the upper bound.
  • stretch One person says 40 to the nearest 10; another says 35 to the nearest whole number. Find the error interval that fits both.

Submission choices: an annotated Scratchpad image, a photographed page, a short slideshow, or a spoken or video walkthrough.
