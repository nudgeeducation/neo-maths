NEO Mathematics: Place Value

Rounding: which is nearest?

Driving Question: Which multiple of 10, 100 or 0.1 is this number closest to?

Lesson type: Core — rounding means finding the nearer marker on a number line; halfway rounds up.

Stepping Stone: round whole numbers and decimals to the nearest 1,000, 100, 10, whole number and 0.1, and explain the answer using the halfway point.

You will need: comparing numbers (Lesson 6), and the optional ✏️ Scratchpad. No calculator needed.

🌿 Connection — The Nearest Marker

🔤 The idea for this lesson

To round a number to the nearest 10, find the two multiples of 10 it lies between (the markers) and choose the one it is nearer to. 347 lies between 340 and 350; it is 7 from 340 and 3 from 350, so it rounds to 350. A number exactly halfway, such as 345, could go either way, so we agree a convention: halfway rounds up.

What to do: slide the purple point along the line, or type a number. Choose what to round to. Tap the marker you think the number rounds to, or choose one of the rounding questions.

What you are trying to notice: where does the answer switch from one marker to the other?

Carry this forward: find the two markers, find the halfway point, and choose the nearer marker. Halfway rounds up.

🌙 Rest — A pause to let it settle

Close enough is a choice. Take a slow breath.

Notice one thing: “about 350 people” and “347 people” are both true. Rounding chooses how much detail to keep.

🍎 Nutrition — What this feeds

Mode: intellectual. The mathematics is the nourishment, so we name honestly what the learning feeds.

What it feeds: today feeds estimation. Rounding lets you check an answer quickly, plan a budget or judge a distance without needing every digit.

Carry one idea forward: two markers, one halfway point, the nearer marker wins.

🛠️ Final Task — The rounding guide

Your task: make a short guide that explains rounding with a number line.

  • Round one number to the nearest 10, 100 and 1,000, drawing the line with both markers and the halfway point each time.
  • Round a decimal to the nearest whole number and to the nearest 0.1.
  • Show a number that is exactly halfway, and explain what happens.
  • stretch Give every whole number that rounds to 80 (to the nearest 10), and explain why the list starts and stops where it does.

Submission choices: an annotated Scratchpad image, a photographed page, a short slideshow, or a spoken or video walkthrough.
