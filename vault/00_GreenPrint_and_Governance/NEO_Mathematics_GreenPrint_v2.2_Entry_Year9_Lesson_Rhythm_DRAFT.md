## Decision — Year 9 (Foundations) runs at 2 lessons a week; Year 10 (Intermediate) stays at 3

The annual rhythm is set per pathway, not once for all pathways:

| Pathway | Lessons per week | Lesson length | Weeks | Annual guided opportunities |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations (Year 9) | **2** | 45 minutes | 38 | **76** |
| Intermediate (Year 10) | 3 | 45 minutes | 38 | 114 |

This replaces the single "3 × 45 minutes × 38 weeks = 114 for every pathway" rhythm recorded in GreenPrint v1.1 and used by the Lesson Architecture Map up to v0.3. The Architecture Map is updated to v0.4 to match.

## Why this entry exists

NEO's 2026–27 term dates give Year 9 two mathematics lessons a week and Year 10 three. Every capacity figure in the planning record assumed three for both, so the Foundations envelope was overstated by 38 lessons a year.

## 2026–27 half-term capacity (from NEO term dates)

| Half-term | Weeks | Year 9 lessons | Year 10 lessons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring HT1 | 5 | 10 | 15 |
| Spring HT2 | 5 | 10 | 15 |
| Summer HT1 | 6 | 12 | 18 |
| Summer HT2 | 6 | 12 | 18 |
| **Spring and Summer** | **22** | **44** | **66** |

Autumn term weeks are to be confirmed and added here.

## Consequences

1. **Plan units to half-terms.** Unit lengths for the remaining Year 9 and Year 10 units are set so that each unit fits inside one half-term where possible, and no unit is split by a holiday.
2. **The Foundations priority envelope is tight.** The Architecture Map marks 71 Foundations opportunities as Essential. Inside 76, that leaves 5 lessons of breathing room, against the 43 assumed before. The Foundations bank needs re-prioritising as each remaining Year 9 unit is blueprinted.
3. **Check the built Autumn units.** Year 9 Autumn has 34 built lessons. Confirm they fit the Autumn weeks at 2 a week, or mark which lessons are Flexible.
4. **Capacity checks use each pathway's own figure.** The Annual Capacity Audit compares Foundations with 76 and Intermediate with 114.

## Unchanged

Lesson length (45 minutes), the six Cornerstones and the 45-minute lesson shape, the responsive envelope (topic counts decided live from evidence), and the principle that the opportunity bank is not a timetable.
