Every lesson has its Practice Companion, Learner Guide and Supporting Adult Guidance. The live site now holds **141 lessons**: 70 in Foundations (54 in Year 9, 16 in Year 7), 69 in Intermediate and 2 in the KS3 Interactive Library. That is 41 more than at the end of October.

## Also this month
- **Year 7, ready for January:** How Much Is a Digit Worth? (place value, 8 lessons, with The Counting House diagnostic), Angles (4) and Perimeter and Area (4), with The Architect's Studio diagnostic.
- **Three more diagnostics:** The Market Hall, The Sorting Office and The Animation Studio. Every Spring unit on both pathways now has one.
- **Unit Check-ins.** Each check-in returns to the unit's diagnostic world, with new locks on each lesson's own ideas. The learner sees where they started and where they are now, room by room, with next steps for anything not yet secure. The supporting adult confirms one label for the unit (Approaching / Developing / Proficiency / Refining Proficiency), with maths conversation cards for when the check-in does not show what a learner really knows. Year 10 check-ins end with an optional Exam Corner of International GCSE-style questions, never recorded. There are no scores and no timers.
- **Results service:** check-ins save to the learner record next to the diagnostic. "Start" in each room is read from the learner's own diagnostic. Only NEO staff can confirm a unit label, from the educator dashboard, and the record shows who confirmed it. The update was installed and checked on 28 November.
- **Scratchpad and calculator** in every diagnostic and check-in. Neither is saved or recorded. The calculator rests in the few locks that test working without one.
- **Geometry Studio** reached v0.6, with a coordinate grid, transformations, enlargement and a calculator for the Transformations unit.
- **iGCSE alignment map v1.2:** 126 of 247 specification points are now taught in live lessons (86 in October), 30 more are partly covered and 15 are treated as Reconnection Routes. Every Foundation tier point in Section 5 (vectors and transformation geometry) is now live, and no Foundation tier Number point is still waiting to be built.
