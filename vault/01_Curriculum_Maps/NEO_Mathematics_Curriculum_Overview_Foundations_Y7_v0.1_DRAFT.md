# NEO Mathematics — Curriculum Overview: Foundations (Year 7)
**Pathway:** Foundations (internal stage code Y7: the start of Key Stage 3) · **Academic year:** 2026–27
**Rhythm (to confirm):** drafted at 2 × 45-minute lessons per week × 38 weeks = 76 guided lesson opportunities, the same as Year 9 Foundations (GreenPrint entry v2.2). Half-terms give 16, 16, 10, 10, 12 and 12 lessons. Each unit is sized to fit inside one half-term.
**Qualification overlay:** National Curriculum in England, Key Stage 3 mathematics, with Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills Mathematics (Levels 1 and 2) where a unit maps to it. The overlay informs the route; it does not define it.
**Sources:** Curriculum Vault Map GreenPrint v0.2 (the ten Year 7 units, their driving questions and Stepping Stones), the KS3 programme of study, and the Year 9 and Year 10 overviews v1.1 for what Year 7 feeds.
**Status (v0.1 DRAFT, 3 November 2026):** for review by Gerry Docherty, curriculum lead, and the Director of NEO. No Year 7 unit is built yet. Lesson titles are provisional until each unit is blueprinted.

## Autumn term — planned

**Autumn HT1 · 16 lessons**

### 1 · How Much Is a Digit Worth? (7 lessons)
Can the same digit represent completely different amounts?
What is a digit worth? · Ten times bigger, ten times smaller · Zero as a placeholder · Partitioning in more than one way · Decimals on a number line · Ordering integers and decimals · One number, many representations
KS3 Number; Working mathematically. Builds on KS2 place value. Becomes the Reconnection Route for place value and magnitude in Years 9 and 10.

### 2 · Calculations That Make Sense (9 lessons)
How can an estimate protect me from a convincing wrong answer?
Adding and subtracting with meaning · Multiplying with arrays and area · Dividing: sharing and grouping · The order of operations · Inverse operations as a check · Estimating before calculating · Negative numbers on a number line · Calculating with negative numbers · Is my answer sensible?
KS3 Number; Functional Skills L1 2–7, 15 and L2 1–2, 12. Becomes the Reconnection Route for four operations, directed numbers and inverse checks.

**Autumn HT2 · 16 lessons**

### 3 · Patterns, Factors and Hidden Structure (8 lessons)
What do numbers reveal when we organise them differently?
Factors and multiples · Prime numbers · Prime factors · Highest common factor and lowest common multiple · Squares, cubes and powers · Patterns that grow · Describing a pattern with a rule · Testing a conjecture
KS3 Number and Algebra. The last three lessons are Year 7's first algebra: a rule for a pattern, written in words, then with a letter.

### 4 · One Amount, Three Languages (8 lessons)
How can fractions, decimals and percentages describe the same share?
Equivalent fractions · Simplifying fractions · Comparing fractions · Fractions as decimals · Percentages as fractions and decimals · Fractions of amounts · Percentages of amounts · Choosing the best representation
KS3 Number; Ratio and proportion; Functional Skills L1 8–17 and L2 4–10.

## Spring term — planned

**Spring HT1 · 10 lessons**

### 5 · Angles Tell Us Why (5 lessons)
Can a diagram prove more than it first shows?
Measuring and drawing angles · Angles on a line and at a point · Angles in a triangle · Properties of triangles and quadrilaterals · Because–therefore: justifying a missing angle
KS3 Geometry and measures. Builds the habit of giving a reason for every step, which Year 10 Circle Theorems relies on.

### 6 · Perimeter and Area: Same Shape Story? (5 lessons)
Can two shapes use the same boundary but hold different space?
Perimeter or area? · Area of rectangles and triangles · Area of parallelograms and trapezia · Composite shapes · Metric units and design choices
KS3 Geometry and measures; Functional Skills L1 20, 22 and L2 16.

**Spring HT2 · 10 lessons**

### 7 · Relationships That Scale (10 lessons)
How can relationships stay the same when quantities change?
Ratio notation · Simplifying ratios · Part–part and part–whole · Ratios and fractions · Sharing in a ratio · Scale factors · Scaling a recipe · Scale diagrams · Proportion problems · Putting it together
KS3 Ratio, proportion and rates of change; a bridge to 4MA1 1.7; Functional Skills L1 17 and L2 11. The KS3 Library ratio interactive is the reference prototype.

## Summer term — planned

**Summer HT1 · 12 lessons**

### 8 · Coordinates and Moves (6 lessons)
Can a shape change position without changing who it is?
Plotting coordinates · All four quadrants · Translations · Reflections in a mirror line · Coordinates and transformations · What stays the same?
KS3 Geometry and measures; Algebra. Uses numbered, labelled axes throughout (GreenPrint v2.3).

### 9 · Averages and Fair Shares (6 lessons)
When is the mean fair, and when is another summary better?
Mode, median and range · The mean as a fair share · Choosing the best average · Bar charts and pictograms · Pie charts · Outliers and what they do
KS3 Statistics; Functional Skills L1 27–29 and L2 23–25.

**Summer HT2 · 12 lessons**

### 10 · Chance, Experiments and Fairness (6 lessons)
How fair is a game after only ten trials?
The probability scale · Listing outcomes · Calculating simple probabilities · Running an experiment · Experiment and theory · Is it fair? Sample size
KS3 Probability; Functional Skills L1 30–31 and L2 26–27.

### End-of-year review and celebration (6 lessons)
A look back across the year, chosen by the learner, ending with a portfolio celebration. The approach to assessment follows the decision on Unit Check-ins.

## Assessment
- **Diagnostic assessment — one per unit** (GreenPrint entry v2.4): a themed expedition before Lesson 1 of each unit, with no scores.
- **End-of-unit assessment:** the proposed Unit Check-in (draft GreenPrint v2.5, awaiting the Director's decision).
- **Reporting scale (confirmed 25 October 2026):** Approaching Proficiency, Developing Proficiency, Proficiency, Refining Proficiency.

## Points to confirm
1. **Rhythm.** Drafted at 2 lessons a week (76 a year), as Year 9. At 3 a week (114), each unit could grow by about half, with more room for Reconnection.
2. **Timing.** A learner of Year 7 age enrolled in November 2026 and is unlikely to begin subject-specific work before January 2027; no start date is confirmed. To be ready, Unit 1 (with its diagnostic) and Unit 2 are built first, by early January, which matches the milestone schedule. That covers the first 16 lessons, about eight weeks at two a week. Until a start date is set, the learner can use the KS3 Interactive Library and Reconnection Routes. The unit diagnostics show where to begin, so the learner does not have to start at Unit 1.
3. **Algebra.** The KS3 programme expects algebraic notation and simple equations early. The Vault Map places them in Year 8 (Algebra as a Language of Structure; Equations), with Year 7's pattern rules in Unit 3 as the bridge. Confirm, or add a short Year 7 algebra unit in Summer HT2 in place of part of the review.
4. **Diagnostics and check-ins.** Confirm whether each unit's diagnostic and check-in sessions sit inside its lesson count or alongside it.
5. **Year 8.** The Year 8 overview (December milestone) will follow the same format, so that Years 7, 8 and 9 read as one route.
