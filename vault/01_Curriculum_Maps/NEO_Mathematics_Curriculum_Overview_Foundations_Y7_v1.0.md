# NEO Mathematics — Curriculum Overview: Foundations (Year 7)
**Pathway:** Foundations (internal stage code Y7: the start of Key Stage 3) · **Academic year:** 2026–27
**Rhythm:** 2 × 45-minute sessions per week × 38 weeks = 76 guided sessions, the same as Year 9 Foundations (GreenPrint entry v2.2; confirmed by Gerry Docherty, 12 November 2026). Half-terms give 16, 16, 10, 10, 12 and 12 sessions. Each unit fits inside one half-term.
**Sessions include assessment:** every unit's diagnostic and Unit Check-in are counted inside its half-term's sessions, as in the units already built (confirmed 28 November 2026). Where two units share a half-term, they share one diagnostic and one check-in.
**Qualification overlay:** National Curriculum in England, Key Stage 3 mathematics, with Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills Mathematics (Levels 1 and 2) where a unit maps to it. The overlay informs the route; it does not define it.
**Sources:** Curriculum Vault Map GreenPrint v0.2 (the ten Year 7 units, their driving questions and Stepping Stones), the KS3 programme of study, and the Year 9 overview v1.2 and Year 10 overview v1.3 for what Year 7 feeds.
**Status (v1.0, 28 November 2026):** confirmed by Gerry Docherty, curriculum lead, for the Director of NEO. Units 1, 5 and 6 are built and live, each with its diagnostic. Lesson titles for the other units are provisional until each unit is blueprinted. See *Changes in v1.0*.

## The route this year: a learner joining in January
NEO's Year 7 learner is expected to begin in January 2027, so this is the route in use for 2026–27 (confirmed 28 November 2026). It has 44 sessions before the summer: 10 + 10 in Spring and 12 + 12 in Summer. Taking the units in their usual order would mean four number units in a row, then more number at the start of Year 8. This route keeps the number spine but alternates the strands. Learner-facing material never names a year group, so units can be arranged to suit each learner.

| Half-term | Sessions | Strand | Units | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spring HT1 | 10 | Number | Unit 1 · How Much Is a Digit Worth? (8 lessons, The Counting House diagnostic, check-in) | Built |
| Spring HT2 | 10 | Geometry and measures | Unit 5 · Angles Tell Us Why and Unit 6 · Perimeter and Area (4 + 4 lessons, The Architect's Studio diagnostic, one check-in) | Built |
| Summer HT1 | 12 | Number | Unit 2 · Calculations That Make Sense (6 lessons, diagnostic, check-in), then 4 flexible sessions for the Reconnection Routes the diagnostics point to | Planned |
| Summer HT2 | 12 | Statistics and probability | Unit 9 · Averages and Fair Shares and Unit 10 · Chance, Experiments and Fairness (4 + 4 lessons, one diagnostic, one check-in), then 2 sessions looking back across the year and celebrating the portfolio | Planned |

**Why this order:** place value comes first, because everything rests on it. The angle, area and data units need only arithmetic of KS2 level, which Unit 1 has just strengthened, and each unit's diagnostic shows any gap early. *Calculations* comes before the data units, which use it most. The four flexible sessions in Summer HT1 give time to return to anything the year's diagnostics and check-ins show is not yet secure.

**Carried into Year 8,** so that the learner meets no extra separate number units:
- Unit 3 (patterns and factors) opens *Algebra as a Language of Structure*.
- Unit 4 (fractions, decimals and percentages) opens *Percent Change and the Story of Growth*.
- Unit 7 (ratio) leads into *Rates, Best Value and Direct Proportion*.
- Unit 8 (coordinates) leads into *Lines Tell Stories of Change*.

The Year 8 overview (December) will build these in as short opening lessons or Reconnection Routes, keeping Year 8's own balance of strands.

**76 sessions:** 58 lessons, 16 diagnostic and check-in sessions (eight units or unit pairs) and 2 sessions looking back. By half-term: 16 + 16 + 10 + 10 + 12 + 12.
**Why the order differs from the Vault Map:** counting each diagnostic and check-in inside the half-term leaves room in Autumn HT1 for Unit 1 (10 sessions) and one short unit (6). *Coordinates and Moves* fits there: it needs only the number work of Unit 1, and it brings geometry into the first half-term, so the strands alternate as in the January route. *One Amount, Three Languages* moves to Spring HT2 and *Relationships That Scale* to Summer HT1, so fractions still come before ratio. The ten units, their driving questions and their Stepping Stones are unchanged.
**Built:** Unit 1 (8 lessons, with *The Counting House* diagnostic) and Units 5 and 6 (4 lessons each, sharing *The Architect's Studio* diagnostic), all live on the NEO Maths site. **Planned:** the other seven units, and the check-ins for the built units (December).
**Already available to reuse:** the KS3 Interactive Library's place-value and ratio interactives (live) can anchor Units 1 and 7.

## Autumn term — planned

**Autumn HT1 · 16 sessions**

### 1 · How Much Is a Digit Worth? (8 lessons, built; diagnostic: The Counting House)
Can the same digit represent completely different amounts?
What is a digit worth? · Ten times bigger, ten times smaller · Zero as a placeholder · Beyond the point: tenths, hundredths, thousandths · Partitioning in more than one way · Which is larger? · Rounding: which is nearest? · One number, many representations
KS3 Number; Working mathematically. Builds on KS2 place value. Becomes the Reconnection Route for place value and magnitude in Years 9 and 10.

### 8 · Coordinates and Moves (4 lessons)
Can a shape change position without changing who it is?
Plotting coordinates in all four quadrants · Translations · Reflections in a mirror line · What stays the same?
KS3 Geometry and measures; Algebra. Uses numbered, labelled axes throughout (GreenPrint v2.3).

**Autumn HT2 · 16 sessions**

### 2 · Calculations That Make Sense (6 lessons)
How can an estimate protect me from a convincing wrong answer?
Adding and subtracting with meaning · Multiplying and dividing: arrays, sharing and grouping · The order of operations · Inverse operations and estimates as checks · Negative numbers on a number line · Calculating with negative numbers
KS3 Number; Functional Skills L1 2–7, 15 and L2 1–2, 12. Becomes the Reconnection Route for four operations, directed numbers and inverse checks.

### 3 · Patterns, Factors and Hidden Structure (6 lessons)
What do numbers reveal when we organise them differently?
Factors and multiples · Prime numbers and prime factors · Highest common factor and lowest common multiple · Squares, cubes and powers · Patterns that grow, and a rule to describe them · Testing a conjecture
KS3 Number and Algebra. The pattern lessons are Year 7's first algebra: a rule for a pattern, written in words, then with a letter. Algebraic notation and simple equations follow in Year 8, which this unit opens (*Algebra as a Language of Structure*; *Equations*), as the Vault Map plans (confirmed 28 November 2026).

## Spring term — Spring HT1 built

**Spring HT1 · 10 sessions**

### 5 · Angles Tell Us Why (4 lessons, built; diagnostic: The Architect's Studio, shared with Unit 6)
Can a diagram prove more than it first shows?
Measuring and drawing angles · Angles on a line and at a point · Angles in a triangle · Because–therefore: quadrilaterals and missing angles
KS3 Geometry and measures; Functional Skills L1 20, 22 and L2 22. Builds the habit of giving a reason for every step, which Year 10 Circle Theorems relies on.

### 6 · Perimeter and Area: Same Shape Story? (4 lessons, built; diagnostic: The Architect's Studio, shared with Unit 5)
Can two shapes use the same boundary but hold different space?
Perimeter or area? · Area of rectangles and triangles · Area of parallelograms and trapezia · Composite shapes and design choices
KS3 Geometry and measures; Functional Skills L1 16, 18 and L2 16. Metric units (m and cm) are part of the last lesson, and of Unit 1's measures lesson.

**Spring HT2 · 10 sessions — planned**

### 4 · One Amount, Three Languages (8 lessons)
How can fractions, decimals and percentages describe the same share?
Equivalent fractions · Simplifying fractions · Comparing fractions · Fractions as decimals · Percentages as fractions and decimals · Fractions of amounts · Percentages of amounts · Choosing the best representation
KS3 Number; Ratio and proportion; Functional Skills L1 8–17 and L2 4–10.

## Summer term — planned

**Summer HT1 · 12 sessions**

### 7 · Relationships That Scale (10 lessons)
How can relationships stay the same when quantities change?
Ratio notation · Simplifying ratios · Part–part and part–whole · Ratios and fractions · Sharing in a ratio · Scale factors · Scaling a recipe · Scale diagrams · Proportion problems · Putting it together
KS3 Ratio, proportion and rates of change; a bridge to 4MA1 1.7; Functional Skills L1 17 and L2 11. The KS3 Library ratio interactive is the reference prototype.

**Summer HT2 · 12 sessions**

### 9 · Averages and Fair Shares (4 lessons; one diagnostic and check-in shared with Unit 10)
When is the mean fair, and when is another summary better?
Mode, median and range · The mean as a fair share, and choosing the best average · Bar charts, pictograms and pie charts · Outliers and what they do
KS3 Statistics; Functional Skills L1 27–29 and L2 23–25.

### 10 · Chance, Experiments and Fairness (4 lessons; shared with Unit 9)
How fair is a game after only ten trials?
The probability scale and listing outcomes · Calculating simple probabilities · Experiment and theory · Is it fair? Sample size
KS3 Probability; Functional Skills L1 30–31 and L2 26–27.

### Looking back and celebration (2 sessions)
A look back across the year, chosen by the learner, ending with a portfolio celebration.

## Assessment
- **Diagnostic assessment, one per unit or unit pair** (GreenPrint entry v2.4): a themed expedition before the first lesson, with no scores. Counted inside the half-term's sessions.
- **Unit Check-in at the end of each unit or unit pair:** it returns to the diagnostic's world, so the learner sees where they started and where they are now, room by room. The supporting adult confirms one label for the unit. Counted inside the half-term's sessions. The Year 9 and Year 10 check-ins are built; the Director's review of the format is pending.
- **Reporting scale (confirmed 25 October 2026):** Approaching Proficiency, Developing Proficiency, Proficiency, Refining Proficiency.
- **Scratchpad and calculator** are available in every diagnostic and check-in; neither is saved or recorded.
