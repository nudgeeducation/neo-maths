# NEO Mathematics — Curriculum Overview: Foundations (Year 9)
**Pathway:** Foundations (internal stage code Y9 — Year 9 bridge with Year 7/8 Reconnection) · **Academic year:** 2026–27
**Rhythm:** 2 × 45-minute lessons per week × 38 weeks = 76 guided lesson opportunities (GreenPrint entry v2.2). Spring and Summer 2026–27 give 44 lessons: 10, 10, 12 and 12 by half-term. Each remaining unit is sized to fit inside one half-term.
**Qualification overlay:** National Curriculum KS3, bridging to Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) Foundation tier. The overlay informs the route; it does not define it.
**Sources:** Lesson Architecture Map GreenPrint v0.4, the 4MA1 specification and the iGCSE alignment map for the planned route; the live site and Google Classroom for the built units. Where they differ, the built lessons are the record.
**Status (v1.0, 14 October 2026):** Confirmed by Gerry Docherty, curriculum lead. Unit order matches Google Classroom and the live site. Autumn is built and live; Spring and Summer are planned to the 2026–27 term dates; lesson counts for planned units may flex when each unit is blueprinted.

**Built so far:** 34 lessons across 5 units, each with its Practice Companion, Learner Guide and Supporting Adult Guidance.
**Planned:** 44 lessons, 10 + 10 + 12 + 12 across the four Spring and Summer half-terms.
**Changes from the Architecture Map:** transformation geometry joins Similarity and Scale in Unit 8; Unit 10 covers Foundation probability and sets, leaving tree diagrams and conditional probability to Year 10; Unit 11 is new and gathers the remaining Foundation tier points.

## Autumn term — built and live

### 1 · Accuracy, Powers and Scale (5 lessons)
How precisely do we need to know a number, and how do we handle the very large and very small?
Rounding to Significant Figures · Estimation · Using Index Laws · Write and Order Standard Form · Calculating with Standard Form

### 2 · Pythagorean Theorem (4 lessons)
The hidden relationship between the sides of a right-angled triangle, and how to use it.
Right Triangles and Hidden Squares · Finding the Hypotenuse · Finding a Shorter Side · Geometrical Problems

### 3 · 2D and 3D Shapes (10 lessons)
From the properties of flat shapes to the surface and space of solids.
Analysing 2D and 3D Properties · Angles in Polygons · Circumference of a Circle · Area of a Circle · Reasoning from Nets · Platonic Solids · Surface Area · Cross-sections · Volume · Converting spatial units

### 4 · Quadratics (8 lessons)
What happens when change itself changes: from growing patterns to the parabola.
Growing patterns · The second difference · The rule behind the sequence · The expression behind it · Meet the parabola · Stretch and reflect · Slide it across · Reading real parabolas

### 5 · Constructions, Loci and Bearings (7 lessons)
Precise drawing with compass and straight edge, and the paths and directions it describes.
The compass and the circle · Shapes that hold their form · The perpendicular bisector · The angle bisector · Loci · Bearings · Putting it together — scale plans

## Spring term — planned

**Spring HT1 · 10 lessons**

### 6 · Inequalities (4 lessons)
Where does a solution stop, and how do we show a whole range of answers at once?
Inequality symbols and double-ended inequalities · Solving linear inequalities · Showing solutions on a number line · Inequalities as regions on a graph
4MA1 2.8A–E. Year 10 met this content in two lessons of its Equations unit; Year 9 takes four, with more time for representation.

### 7 · Formulae and Rearranging (6 lessons)
How does a formula capture a relationship, and how do I turn it round to find what I need?
Letters as unknowns and variables · Substituting positive, negative and fractional values · Formulae from words and diagrams · Deriving a formula · Changing the subject · Using and checking formulae in context
4MA1 2.1A–B, 2.2A, 2.3A–F. Reconnection Routes: substitution · one- and two-step equations.

**Spring HT2 · 10 lessons**

### 8 · Geometry: Transformations, Similarity and Scale (10 lessons)
What changes and what stays the same when a shape moves, turns, flips or grows?
Reflection · Rotation · Translation and column vectors · What transformations keep: congruence · Describing transformations fully · Enlargement, including fractional scale factors · Similar shapes · Missing lengths in similar shapes · Maps and scale drawings · Area scale factor and putting it together
4MA1 5.2A–M, 4.2F–G, 4.11A–B. Enlargement is the bridge from transformations to similarity. Lesson 9 reconnects to Constructions Lesson 7 (scale plans).

## Summer term — planned

**Summer HT1 · 12 lessons**

### 9 · Sampling, Bias and What We Can Infer (6 lessons)
What can a sample really tell us, and when should we doubt a claim?
Planning a statistical enquiry · Sampling and bias · Tables and charts, including two-way tables · Averages and range · Grouped data: modal class and estimated mean · Scatter graphs and what we can infer
4MA1 6.1A–C, 6.2A–D. Reconnection Routes: averages and range · comparing distributions.

### 10 · Probability, Sets and Venn Diagrams (6 lessons)
How likely is it, and how do sets help us count the possibilities?
The language and scale of probability · Sample spaces and listing outcomes · The complement and mutually exclusive events · Sets and Venn diagrams · Probabilities from Venn diagrams · Experimental probability and expected frequency
4MA1 1.5A–E, 6.3A–J. Tree diagrams and conditional probability are Higher tier and are taught in Year 10.

**Summer HT2 · 12 lessons**

### 11 · Measures, Sequences and Coordinates (7 lessons)
Gathers the Foundation tier points that no other unit reaches.
Linear sequences and the nth term · Coordinates from geometry and midpoints · Symmetry in 2D shapes · Reading scales and estimating measures · Time and timetables · Speed, distance and time · Compound measures: density and pressure
4MA1 1.10C, 3.1C, 3.3D–E, 4.3A, 4.4A–C, 4.4F–G.
