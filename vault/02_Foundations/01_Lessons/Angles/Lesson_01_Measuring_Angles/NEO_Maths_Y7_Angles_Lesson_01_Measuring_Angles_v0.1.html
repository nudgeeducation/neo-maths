🌿 Connection — The Turning Arm

🔤 The idea for this lesson

An angle is an amount of turn, measured in degrees (°). A full turn is 360°, so a quarter turn, a right angle, is 90°, and half a turn, a straight line, is 180°. Angles less than 90° are acute; between 90° and 180° they are obtuse; more than 180° they are reflex. A protractor has two scales. Estimate first, and the estimate tells you which scale to read.

What to do: drag the yellow handle to turn the arm, or use the buttons (or the arrow keys). Lay on the protractor to measure. Try changing the length of the arms, and turning the whole drawing.

What you are trying to notice: what changes the size of an angle, and what does not?

↻ Turn the arm

On the protractor, read the red scale: it starts at 0 on the base arm.
