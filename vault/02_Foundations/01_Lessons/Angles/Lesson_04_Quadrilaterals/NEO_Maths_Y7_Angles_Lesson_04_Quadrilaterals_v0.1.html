NEO Mathematics: Angles Tell Us Why

Because–therefore: quadrilaterals and missing angles

Driving Question: Can a diagram prove more than it first shows?

🌿 Connection — The Reason Chain

🔤 The idea for this lesson

A quadrilateral has four sides and four angles. One diagonal splits it into two triangles, so its angles add up to 180° + 180° = 360°. Special quadrilaterals have extra facts: a parallelogram's opposite angles are equal; a rectangle's angles are all right angles; a kite has one pair of equal opposite angles. When one fact leads to the next, write each step with its reason: because … therefore …

What to do: drag the corners to make any four-sided shape (the angles stay whole numbers). Watch the total. Then draw the diagonal. Try the special shapes too.

What you are trying to notice: why is the total always the same, and what extra facts do the special shapes have?
