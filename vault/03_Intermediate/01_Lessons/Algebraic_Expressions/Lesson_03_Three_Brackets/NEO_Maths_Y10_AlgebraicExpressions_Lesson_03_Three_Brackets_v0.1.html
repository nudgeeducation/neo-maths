Expanding three brackets

Driving Question: What happens when three brackets multiply?

Lesson type: Core — three brackets, two stages, eight products.

Stepping Stone: expand three brackets in two stages, including negative terms, coefficients and the cube of a bracket; explain why there are 8 products before collecting; and use the structure of (x + a)(x + b)(x + c) to check an expansion quickly.

You will need: expanding two brackets with the grid, and multiplying negative numbers — and the optional ✏️ Scratchpad. No calculator needed.

🌿 Connection — Powers meet brackets

🔤 The idea for this lesson

To expand three brackets, expand two of them first, then multiply the result by the third. Along the way, powers of x add: x × x² = x³. The answer is a cubic expression, with an x³ term.

What to do: work out each product or expansion. Use the √ Symbols button for ² and ³.

What you are trying to notice: multiply the numbers, and add the powers of x.

✖️ Warm up

Carry this forward: x × x² = x³, and 2x × 3x² = 6x³.

🚶 Movement — Pick one from each

What to do: every product in the expansion takes one term from each bracket. Pick a term from each of the three brackets and see the product. Find all the different ways.

What you are trying to notice: each bracket gives 2 choices, so there are 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 products before collecting.

🌳 (x + 1)(x + 2)(x + 3)

Carry this forward: 8 products: x³, three x² terms, three x terms and one number. Then collect.

🪞 Reflection — The two-stage grid

What to do: Stage 1: expand the first two brackets with a 2 by 2 grid, and collect. Stage 2: your answer goes down the side of a new grid, with the third bracket as the column headings. Fill the cells and collect again. For all-positive brackets, open the box to see the expansion as a volume.

What you are trying to notice: stage 2 is just a bigger grid. Like terms sit on the diagonals, so collecting is quick.

🌙 Rest — A pause to let it settle

Three brackets, eight pieces, four kinds of piece. Take a slow breath.

Notice one thing: 1, 3, 3, 1 — the numbers in (x + 1)³ = x³ + 3x² + 3x + 1 count the pieces of each kind in the box.

You can pause here and return whenever you are ready.

🍎 Nutrition — What this feeds

Mode: intellectual. The mathematics is the nourishment, so we name honestly what the learning feeds.

What it feeds: today feeds the satisfaction of scaling up a method you already trust. You did not need a new rule for three brackets — just the grid, used twice. Building big things from small, reliable steps is how most hard problems are solved.

Carry one idea forward: when something looks new, ask which old method it is really made of.

🛠️ Final Task — Eight pieces

Your task: show that you can expand three brackets and check your answer.

  • Expand and simplify (x + 2)(x + 3)(x + 4), showing both stages.
  • Expand and simplify (x − 1)(x + 2)(x − 3) and (x + 3)³.
  • Explain why there are 8 products before collecting.
  • Check each answer using the number term and the x² coefficient.
  • stretch Expand (2x − 1)(x + 3)(x − 2), and explain how the check changes when one bracket starts with 2x.
