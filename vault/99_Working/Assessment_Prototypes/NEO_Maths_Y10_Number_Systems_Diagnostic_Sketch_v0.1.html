Before you set off

Number Systems and Calculator Judgement · Year 10 · Spring term

This is not a test. There is no clock, no score and nothing to lose. It takes about 15 minutes, and you can stop and come back.

It's like packing for a journey. Eight short questions help us work out what you're already carrying, and what's worth packing before we start the unit.

For each question, first tell us how it feels, then have a go. "This is new to me" is a perfectly good answer — it tells us where to start.

What is saved, and who can see it?

Only your answers and how each question felt to you. Nothing else: not how long you took, not how many times you tried, not what you clicked.

Your supporting adult sees exactly the same summary you see at the end. You can look at it, change an answer, and run it again whenever you like.
