Welcome to the Observatory

This is an expedition, not a test. There is no clock, no score and nothing to lose. Take as long as you like.

Each room has a locked door. The right answer is the key. If a lock looks unfamiliar, every room also has a side passage that leads on just the same. Taking it is a good choice: it tells us what to help you with.

What is saved, and who can see it?

Only your answers and how each lock looked to you. Nothing else: not how long you took, not how many tries. Your supporting adult sees exactly the floor plan you see at the end.

In this sketch, nothing is saved at all.

If moving between rooms feels uncomfortable, choose Still mode: rooms change with a gentle fade and nothing moves. Or choose Flat view for just the questions, with no 3D at all. You can switch at any time from the top bar.
