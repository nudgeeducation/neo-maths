## About this summary
- **No scores, marks, percentages or rankings.** NEO reports where each learner started and how far they have come, using the same information the learner sees.
- **What is recorded** for each check-in: how each idea felt to the learner, the outcome (secure, getting there, not yet), a short note on any common slip, and the adult's label. Nothing about timings or numbers of attempts.
- **Where it is kept:** NEO's Google Workspace. Attendance and engagement are reported separately by NEO.
