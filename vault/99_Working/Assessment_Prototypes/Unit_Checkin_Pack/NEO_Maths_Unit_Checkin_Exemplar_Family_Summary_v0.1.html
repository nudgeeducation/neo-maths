NEO uses four words to describe progress: Approaching Proficiency, Developing Proficiency, Proficiency and Refining Proficiency. They describe where a learner is now, not a final grade.

In Alex's own words

"I'm proud I can expand three brackets now. I didn't think I'd get the proof one."

How you can help at home

  • Ask Alex to show you the function machine from the last lesson, and try feeding it a number together.
  • Ask: "What was the proof you were proud of?" Explaining it is great practice.
  • Be curious rather than testing: "How did you work that out?" helps more than "Is that right?"
  • If Alex mentions finding something hard, that's normal and useful. It's already on Alex's next-steps list.

Want to know more?

Alex's supporting adult is happy to talk through this summary. There are no marks or rankings: NEO focuses on where each learner started and how far they've come.
