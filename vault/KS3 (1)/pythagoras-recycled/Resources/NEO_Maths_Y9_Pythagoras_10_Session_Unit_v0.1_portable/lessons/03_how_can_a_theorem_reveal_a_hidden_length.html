How can a theorem reveal a hidden length?

Driving Question: How can a theorem reveal a length we cannot see?
Stepping Stones
  • choose a route from the role of the unknown side
  • find a hypotenuse or shorter side
  • sense-check a calculated length
📘 Learner Guide 🤝 Supporting Adult Guidance
🌿 Connection — What changes when the unknown side changes?
What to do
  1. Choose whether the unknown is the hypotenuse or a shorter side.
  2. Enter the known lengths.
  3. Before pressing Next, predict whether the route will add or subtract.
  4. Reveal one reasoning step at a time.
What you are trying to notice

The role of the unknown side changes the algebraic route.

What to carry forward

Always identify the hypotenuse before choosing an operation.

What to carry forward

What to carry forward

🪞 Reflection — What can an error reveal?
What to do
  1. Read this error: '13² + 5² = x²' when x is a shorter side.
  2. Locate the hypotenuse.
  3. Explain which quantity should stand alone.
  4. Correct the equation.
What you are trying to notice

Errors can reveal a confusion about side roles before they reveal an arithmetic problem.

What to carry forward

Check the geometry before checking the calculator.

Error to diagnose: 13² + 5² = x²
🌙 Rest — One decision at a time
What to do
  1. Cover every step except 'identify the hypotenuse'.
  2. Do that step only.
  3. Then uncover the substitution step.
  4. Continue one decision at a time.
What you are trying to notice

You do not need to hold the whole algorithm in working memory.

What to carry forward

One correct structural decision can unlock the next.

🥣 Nutrition — Building mathematical agency
What to do
  1. Choose the habit being nourished: method choice, precision, inverse-operation awareness or sense-checking.
  2. Name one place you used it.
What you are trying to notice

This session uses intellectual nourishment.

What to carry forward

A reliable method is a form of mathematical agency.

🛠 Final Task — Two hidden-length routes
What to do
  1. Solve one unknown-hypotenuse example and one unknown-shorter-side example.
  2. Label the hypotenuse before calculating.
  3. Show the equation, the operation and the square root step.
  4. Add one sense-check sentence.
What you are trying to notice

Your two solutions should make the different routes visible.

What to carry forward
