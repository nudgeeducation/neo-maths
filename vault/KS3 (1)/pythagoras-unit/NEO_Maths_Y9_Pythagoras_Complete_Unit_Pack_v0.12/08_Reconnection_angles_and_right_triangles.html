🌱 Open Learner Guide 📘 Open Supporting Adult Guidance 🧭 Unit Route
🌿 Connection — What mathematical structure matters today?

Pythagoras requires a right-angled triangle. Angle facts can reveal the right angle even when the diagram does not mark it directly.

Stepping Stone focus: recognise right angles; use triangle angle facts.

🚶 Movement — How does the mathematical idea change, move or unfold?

🔓 Angle Unlocker

🎨 Creativity — How can I make or represent the idea in my own way?

Annotate a triangle to show how angle facts prove a 90° angle.

You may draw, annotate, type, speak to a Supporting Adult or use a Sticky Note.

🪞 Reflection — What changed in my mathematical thinking?

Pause and notice what your thinking is doing, not only whether you reached an answer.

Reflection prompt: What changed in your understanding of recognise right angles; use triangle angle facts during this session?

Mastery reflection: Can you now use angle facts to identify or justify a right-angled triangle?

You may respond with a sentence, a sketch, a Sticky Note, a spoken explanation or an annotated Scratchpad image.

🌙 Rest — What helps me pause, regulate and return?

Known angles first. Total 180°. Find the missing angle. Then decide whether Pythagoras is available.

This is another targeted return route. Reconnect the angle fact, then return to the theorem-selection lesson.

Regulation choice: close other panels, use one colour, use paper, pause, or return later.

🥣 Nutrition — What sustains the learning?

Sometimes the key information is not the thing we are trying to calculate. It is the condition that allows a method to be used.

Driving Question: Which angle facts unlock the diagram?

Nutrition Cornerstone lens: In this session, nourishment means noticing what helps mathematical thinking remain usable and sustainable. The context is not forced into food or health; instead, ask what information, support, pacing or representation helps you keep going.

Try this sentence: “My mathematical thinking was better sustained when…”

🛠 Final Task — Evidence for today

Produce one annotated diagram that proves a triangle is right-angled.

Mastery check: I can use angle facts to identify or justify a right-angled triangle.

Evidence opportunity: Uses angle facts sufficiently to identify a right triangle and return to Pythagoras.
