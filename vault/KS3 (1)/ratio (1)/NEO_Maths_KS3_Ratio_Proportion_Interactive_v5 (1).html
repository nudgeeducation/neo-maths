🌿 Connection - What is connected to what?

Ratio shows a relationship

A ratio compares quantities. In a recipe, ingredients are connected. When the number of people changes, each ingredient must change by the same multiplier.

🌙 Rest - Where is the pause?

Calm proportional reasoning routine

  1. Find the starting amount.
  2. Find the new amount.
  3. Work out the multiplier.
  4. Use that multiplier for every connected quantity.
  5. Check whether the relationship still makes sense.

Helpful self-talk: “I do not need to solve the whole recipe at once. I can find the multiplier first.”

🥣 Nutrition - How does this maths help us sustain life?

Ratio can support decisions about quantities, access, cost and preparing enough for a group. Maths helps us compare and plan; people still make the human judgement.
