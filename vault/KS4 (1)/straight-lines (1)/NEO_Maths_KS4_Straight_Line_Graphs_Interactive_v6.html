NEO Mathematics: Straight-Line Graphs

Driving Question: Can a line tell the story of change?

Lesson focus: interpreting and drawing straight-line graphs using y = mx + c.

Stepping Stones: connect equations, tables and graphs; identify gradient as rate of change; identify the y-intercept as starting value; interpret positive, negative and zero gradients; write equations of straight lines; and explain linear models in context.

NEO Cornerstones: Connection, Movement, Creativity, Reflection, Rest and Nutrition.

🌿 Connection - How are equation, table and graph connected?

y = mx + c

  • m is the gradient: the change in y for each increase of 1 in x.
  • c is the y-intercept: the value of y when x = 0.

Start + Change is a useful way to read a line.

🌙 Rest - Where is the pause?

Start. Change. Check.

  1. Start: find c and the y-intercept.
  2. Change: find m and the rate of change.
  3. Check: use a table, point or story to test the relationship.

Helpful self-talk: “I do not need to understand the whole graph at once. I can find the start, then find the change.”

🥣 Nutrition - How can linear models support real choices?

A community lunch might have a fixed room cost and a cost per person. The gradient can represent cost per person; the intercept can represent the fixed cost. The line supports planning, but people still decide what is fair and realistic.
